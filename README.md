# OutBound 🚀
A compact, custom-built handheld ESP32-S3 cyberdeck featuring a 2.4-inch TFT display, an 8x8 LED matrix for notifications, and tactile physical controls—designed from scratch with hand-drawn paper schematics and 3D-printed enclosures.

---

## 🛠️ Hardware Specifications & Dimensions
* **Dimensions:** 9.2 cm x 6.7 cm (Compact handheld form factor)
* **Microcontroller:** UG LAND INDIA ESP32-S3 WROOM N16R8 CAM Board
* **Display:** 2.4-inch SPI TFT Display (ILI9341)
* **Status & Alerts:** MAX7219 8x8 LED Matrix Board
* **Audio:** MAX98357A I2S Audio Amplifier with speaker
* **Power:** 3.7V 2500mAh LiPo Battery with USB-C charging/flashing
* **User Inputs:** 
  * Rotary Thumb Wheel / Encoder
  * Dedicated Push Buttons (Navigation & Home)

## 🛠️ Build Logs

### Log 1: Top Shell & Layout CAD
Spent 7 hours designing and finalizing the custom two-part enclosure for the handheld cyberdeck in Tinkercad. The top shell was constructed on a 92x67mm base plate with a 15mm wall thickness, featuring precise cutouts for the 2.4" TFT display active window (49.4x37.4mm), an 8x8 LED matrix (20.4x20.4mm), dual 12.4mm push buttons, and a front-edge rotary encoder cutout designed to hang slightly off the bottom edge for easy thumb control.

### Log 2: Bottom Tray & Export Preparation
Continued the CAD session by designing and finalizing the matching bottom tray enclosure to house the internal hardware stackup. A 2mm wall thickness was maintained around a hollow interior cavity sized specifically to fit the dual-board PCB stack, LiPo battery, and power routing. Cut a targeted 8.5mm aperture into the bottom floor to align cleanly with the ESP32 camera module lens, and added a side-wall slot engineered for a flat disc thumbwheel potentiometer to protrude by approximately 6mm for easy volume adjustment.
